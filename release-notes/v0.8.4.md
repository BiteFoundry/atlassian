## What's Changed

### Terminal Image Preview Quality & Protocol Detection
- Replaced fixed low-resolution downsampling and stretched aspect-ratio math with terminal cell-geometry sizing and unified protocol detection across Kitty, iTerm2, Sixel, and half-block renderers.

### Native HTML/SVG/Canvas & Embedded Graphics Rendering
- Eliminated raw HTML/SVG/script source leaks in Confluence/Jira HTML previews via pure-Rust `bitefoundry-html` (`resvg 0.50` + `tiny-skia` + `base64`) for inline `<svg>`, `<canvas>`, and `data:image/*` graphics, with an explicit `[o: Browser]` banner for external `<script src>` content.

### Unified Markdown Rendering & `.md` Preview
- Standardized Markdown and ADF conversion on `pulldown-cmark 0.13` via `bitefoundry-html::markdown` and enabled direct `.md` attachment previewing.

### Width-Aware Text Wrapping & Styled Line Wrapping
- Removed the `bg.is_some()` wrap bypass that clipped background-styled lines, and migrated Jira issues, Confluence documents, boards, and search views to display-width-aware styled wrapping (`wrap_styled_spans` / `render_wrapped_table`).

### 6-Tab Refresh/Sync Consistency & Full Mouse/Overlay UX
- Hardened refresh/sync consistency across all 6 Atlassian tabs (Jira, Confluence, Board, Search, etc.) and fixed mouse click/scroll/overlay dispatch interactions.
