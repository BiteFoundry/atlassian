## What's Changed

### Performance Optimization Suite (Network, SQLite/FTS5, TUI & Pixel Pipeline)
- **HTTP/2 + Gzip & Pool Tuning**: Enabled `reqwest` `http2` and `gzip` features and tuned connection pooling/keepalive/`tcp_nodelay` in `src/atlassian/client.rs`.
- **SQLite & FTS5 O(log N) Upgrade**: Added a 4-reader WAL pool, tuned PRAGMAs (`cache_size`, `mmap_size`, `temp_store`, `journal_size_limit`) + `prepare_cached`, aligned `issues_fts`/`pages_fts` to `rowid`-indexed deletes (`O(N²) → O(log N)`), pushed `count_issues_filtered_for_site` to SQL `COUNT(*)`, and offloaded blocking DB work in `serve.rs`/`sync.rs` to `spawn_blocking`.
- **TUI & Pixel Engine**: Eliminated 2-pass `html_preview` cache thrashing, deferred `viewport_pixel_payload` until `plan_transition` emits, added wiki/issue rendered-line caches, promoted 8 uncached `Regex::new` calls to `LazyLock`, upgraded Sixel quantization to flat 32K histogram + 5-5-5 LUT + 2-row `i16` dithering + fast ASCII RLE, switched PNG encoding to `Fast`/`NoFilter` (`fdeflate`), and enabled `opt-level = 3` for codec/pixel crates.
