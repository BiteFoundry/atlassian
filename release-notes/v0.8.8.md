## What's Changed

### Binary Size, Build Diet & ADF Safety Hardening
- **Smaller Release Binaries**: Enabled `opt-level = "s"` and `panic = "abort"` in `[profile.release]`, cutting release binary sizes by ~40% with zero feature loss.
- **ADF & Stack Safety Hardening**: Replaced all 13 non-test `unwrap()` calls in `src/atlassian/body.rs` with typed `BodyEditError` handling, deduplicated Confluence Storage XHTML preprocessing, boxed 5 oversized enum variants (`WireCoreEvent`, `ServerFrame`, `DaemonMode`, `CoreEvent`, `Overlay`), and resolved all Clippy warnings (`-D warnings` clean).
- **Dependency & Packaging Diet**: Upgraded `ratatui` to `0.29`, `dirs` to `6`, and trimmed `bitefoundry-html` `resvg` features; removed unused `crossterm/event-stream`, `reqwest/stream`, `futures`, `hex`, and `uuid`, moved `tempfile` to dev-dependencies, added optional Cargo feature gates (`html-js`, `svg-graphics`, `desktop-notify`), and externalized `assets/demo.gif` to `bitefoundry.dev`.
